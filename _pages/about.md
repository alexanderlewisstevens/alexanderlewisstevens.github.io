<p class="home-kicker">Teaching Assistant Professor of Computer Science at the University of Denver</p>

I teach programming, data structures and algorithms, discrete mathematics, and advanced algorithms. I earned a Ph.D. in mathematics, and my research is in graph theory, discrete curvature, and computational geometry.

Much of my teaching work concerns how students learn to move between working code, formal arguments, and plain-language explanations. I also develop curriculum, support graduate teaching assistants, and mentor independent projects.
